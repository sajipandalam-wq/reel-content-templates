# On-Screen Text Rules

- **Language:** English only, even in Malayalam voiceover reels.
- **Position:** lower third of the frame, never covering the face or the main subject. For avatar reels, key captions go in front of the avatar.
- **Style:** bold, high contrast, 2 to 5 words per line, with a subtle shadow or outline.
- **Timing:** each text stays on screen for at least 1.5 seconds.
- **SFX:** add a whoosh or pop on each new text appearance.

## Example flow

| Section | Text |
|---|---|
| Hook | He had one card… and one dream |
| Middle | Success starts with a single move |
| Ending | Are you ready to drive yours? |
